Portfolio

Proof you can touch

One portfolio piece, shown whole: LUMÉRA — Maison de Parfum, a fictional luxury perfume house built as a single-screen gallery. Five bottles stand in niches along a dark basalt wall; choose one and the camera glides in for a full close-up. The shop works end to end — bag, quantities, a completed checkout — and the prices follow the language: rupiah in Bahasa Indonesia, dollars in English.

  • 01

    A camera move, not a page change

    Opening a perfume runs one continuous 1,500 ms camera zoom into its niche — the detail sheet arrives inside the same screen, with a 01 / 05 stepper to walk the collection.

  • 02

    A shop that really checks out

    Add to bag flies the bottle to the bag; the drawer holds quantities and a subtotal, and checkout validates name, contact, and address before issuing a concept order code.

  • 03

    Prices follow the language

    The five extraits — Cendre d'Or, Pivoine Noire, Sauge de Pierre, Encre Marine, Rouge Braise — are priced in rupiah under Bahasa Indonesia and in dollars under English, at one fixed rate, and the bag total switches with the toggle.

Shown here as stills — the site itself lives on its own page.

What to look for

  • The printed bottle labels — every niche and close-up carries its own name.
  • One wall, five accent colours — the whole page re-lights per perfume.
  • The phone wall is its own portrait render, niches stacked 2 + 3.

      Fit

      Is this the right tool?

      A good fit if…

      Your business needs to be found and contacted from a phone — a company profile, a landing page for one offer, or a small store — and you want it on your own domain, in your name.

      Not the right tool if…

      You need a large platform — member accounts, payments at scale, a custom web app. Carl will say so in the reply rather than sell you a page that cannot carry it.

      What Carl needs from you

      A one-line goal, whatever text and photos you have, and a target date if there is one. No domain or hosting needed beforehand — they are set up in your name as step one.

      FAQ

      Questions, answered

      What does Carl need from me to start?

      A one-line goal, whatever text and photos you already have, and a target date if you have one. Rough drafts are fine — Carl shapes them with you. The full checklist is in the guide to choosing a website service.

      Do I need a domain and hosting first?

      No. If you do not have them yet, Carl sets them up in your name as the first step of the build — the provider costs are yours at the provider's price and are listed in the quote, separate from Carl's work.

      Who owns the website and its accounts?

      You do — domain, hosting, and every account are registered in your name, with the main access in your hands. Carl works in them as a guest; if you ever move on, everything goes with you.
