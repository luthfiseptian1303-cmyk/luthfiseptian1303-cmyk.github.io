Fit

Is this the right tool?

A good fit if…

Your business needs to be found and contacted from a phone — a company profile, a landing page for one offer, or a small store — and you want it on your own domain, in your name.

Not the right tool if…

You need a large platform — member accounts, payments at scale, a custom web app. Carl will say so in the reply rather than sell you a page that cannot carry it.

What Carl needs from you

A one-line goal, whatever text and photos you have, and a target date if there is one. No domain or hosting needed beforehand — they are set up in your name as step one.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What does Carl need from me to start?

A one-line goal, whatever text and photos you already have, and a target date if you have one. Rough drafts are fine — Carl shapes them with you. The full checklist is in the guide to choosing a website service.

Do I need a domain and hosting first?

No. If you do not have them yet, Carl sets them up in your name as the first step of the build — the provider costs are yours at the provider's price and are listed in the quote, separate from Carl's work.

Who owns the website and its accounts?

You do — domain, hosting, and every account are registered in your name, with the main access in your hands. Carl works in them as a guest; if you ever move on, everything goes with you.
