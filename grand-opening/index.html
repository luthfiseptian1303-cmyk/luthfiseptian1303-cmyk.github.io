Our story

Roasted small,
poured with care

AURELO began as a single 5-kilo roaster and a stubborn idea: coffee tastes better when you know the farm, the roast date, and the person pouring it.

Our new Carlton home roasts on site every morning, bakes in the kitchen behind the bar, and keeps twelve seats at the brew bar for anyone who wants to watch the work.

Good to know

Questions

Is opening morning free?

Yes. Entry is free, first pours are on the house until 11 AM, and the first 100 guests take home a bag of beans. After 11, the regular menu applies.

Do I have to RSVP?

No — walk-ins are welcome all day. RSVP only reserves your numbered gift bag and helps us roast the right amount.

Can I bring kids or a dog?

Both, gladly. There is a kids' corner by the window, and the courtyard is dog-friendly with bowls and house-made pup treats.

What if it rains?

The ribbon cutting moves under the awning and the party continues inside. Melbourne weather has never cancelled a coffee yet.
