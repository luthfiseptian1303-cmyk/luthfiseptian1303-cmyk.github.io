08 — Questions

Before you ask

Can I bring my children?

We love them, and the cliff lawn at dusk is an adults-only evening. Babes in arms are welcome at the ceremony; our wedding team can recommend trusted Bali nannies for the night.

Can I take photos?

The ceremony is unplugged — phones away, photographers everywhere. From the reception onwards, shoot everything; the couple's favourite candid wins a bottle of the wedding wine.

What about parking and getting home?

Valet parking at the estate gate from 3:00 PM. Reception shuttles loop to Jimbaran and Pecatu hotels from 10:30 PM, and the desk can call registered taxis all night.

When is the RSVP deadline?

12 May 2027. The lawn seats 120 and the kitchen plans by name — a reply after the deadline may mean a beautiful view and a very small chair.
