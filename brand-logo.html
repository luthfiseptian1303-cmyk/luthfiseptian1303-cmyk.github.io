    Fit

    Is this the right tool?

    A good fit if…

    Your product sits on a shelf or a feed next to competitors, and your current mark is a template, a screenshot, or nothing yet.

    Not the right tool if…

    You need a full brand strategy, naming research, or trademark filing — Carl draws identities; legal and strategy work belongs with specialists, and he will point you there.

    What Carl needs from you

    Your business name as it must appear, where the logo will live (packaging, profile, signage), and anything you already use — even a rough old logo helps.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    What if the first direction does not fit?

    Then Carl asks what exactly does not fit — a shape, a colour, a feeling — and draws the next round from that answer. The number of rounds is agreed up front and written into the quote.

    I already have a logo. Can Carl tidy it up?

    Yes. An existing logo can be cleaned up, redrawn as proper files, and given the versions it is missing — without starting over.

    Exactly which files are mine?

    The finished package: main logo, profile badge, icon, and light/dark versions in SVG and PNG, plus the short usage guide. The files are yours to use for your business — print, packaging, social media, signage.

    Carl reads it himself and replies within a day.
