Your work path

    Questions

    Before you ask

    How much does it cost?+

    It depends on scope. You receive a written price breakdown before work begins, and that figure never changes mid-way without your approval. There are no prices on this page because no two businesses need the same thing.

    How long does it take?+

    The exact schedule is written in the quote, matched to the queue and the level of difficulty. The studio's working capacity is up to 40 hours per week, and Carl keeps you posted at every stage — never silence followed by a sudden delivery.

    Can I request revisions?+

    Yes. The number of revision rounds is agreed up front and written into the quote, so both sides are clear from day one.

    Does the lead system message my leads automatically?+

    Never. The system only prepares follow-up drafts. You review every message yourself, and you press send — no message ever goes out without your approval.

    Can I order more than one service at once?+

    Yes — for example a website with a logo, a promo mini-game, and your leads tidied into one pipeline. Just mention everything in one message; the details are combined into a single quote.

    How is it paid?+

    The payment terms are part of the written quote, agreed before any work begins. Nothing is charged automatically through this website — you always approve the quote first.

    Can I update the site myself afterwards?+

    That depends on what we build, and it is settled in the quote. Every handover includes a walkthrough of how everything works — and if you would rather not touch it, small updates can come back to the studio.
