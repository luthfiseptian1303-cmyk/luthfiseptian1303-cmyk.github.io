    Questions

    Before you ask

    How much does it cost?+

    It depends on scope. You receive a written price breakdown before work begins, and that figure never changes mid-way without your approval. There are no prices on this page because no two businesses need the same thing. As a rule of thumb: small projects are quoted as one fixed figure; larger ones are split into stages in the same written quote.

    How long does it take?+

    The exact schedule is written in the quote, matched to the queue and the level of difficulty. The studio's working capacity is up to 40 hours per week, and Carl keeps you posted at every stage — never silence followed by a sudden delivery.

    Can I request revisions?+

    Yes. The number of revision rounds is agreed up front and written into the quote, so both sides are clear from day one.

    Does the lead system message my leads automatically?+

    Never. The system only prepares follow-up drafts. You review every message yourself, and you press send — no message ever goes out without your approval.

    Can I order more than one service at once?+

    Yes — for example a website with a logo, a promo mini-game, a launch video cut from your own footage, and your leads tidied into one pipeline. Just mention everything in one message; the details are combined into a single quote.

    How is it paid?+

    The payment terms are part of the written quote, agreed before any work begins. Nothing is charged automatically through this website — you always approve the quote first.

    Can I update the site myself afterwards?+

    That depends on what we build, and it is settled in the quote. Every handover includes a walkthrough of how everything works — and if you would rather not touch it, small updates can come back to the studio.

    Who owns the domain, the website, and the accounts?+

    You do. The domain, hosting, and accounts are registered in your name; Carl sets them up and works in them as a guest. If you ever move to someone else, the files, access, and content go with you — nothing is held back.

    What if the schedule slips?+

    You hear it from Carl as soon as he knows — with the reason and the new date. The schedule in your quote is matched to the studio’s real queue, and you get word at every stage, never silence followed by a surprise.

    How is my customers’ data handled?+

    It stays in your accounts and your tools. A lead system is built inside what you already use, follow-ups are drafts until you send them, and Carl keeps no copies of your customer data after handover.

    What if I want to stop mid-project?+

    You can. Everything delivered up to that point stays yours, and how a stop is settled — work done, stages paid — is written in the quote before work begins. No lock-in and no files held hostage.
