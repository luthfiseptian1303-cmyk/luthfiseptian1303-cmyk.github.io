Fit

Is this the right tool?

A good fit if…

Leads already arrive — through chat, forms, or social — but follow-up depends on memory, and warm leads cool down before anyone answers.

Not the right tool if…

You have no lead flow yet, or you need a full CRM migration with a sales team. This system tidies and prioritises an existing flow; it does not invent one.

What Carl needs from you

Your current lead sources, even if today they are only chat threads and a notebook, and the tools you already pay for or use daily.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does the lead system message my leads automatically?

Never. The system only prepares follow-up drafts. You review every message yourself, and you press send — no message ever goes out without your approval.

What do I need to start?

Your current lead sources — even if today they are only chat threads and a notebook. Carl maps them into the pipeline with you, one channel at a time.

Can it be combined with a website?

Yes — a website that feeds the pipeline directly is the most common pairing. Mention both in one message and they are combined into a single quote.

What happens to my leads' data?

It stays in your accounts and your tools — the pipeline is built inside what you already use. Carl keeps no copies of your customer data after handover, and follow-ups remain drafts until you send them.
