01 · Our Story

Roasted in a warehouse.
Served on Crown Street.

The first extraction in the new room — Crown St, 6 AM

For three years KOVRA was a drum roaster in a Marrickville warehouse and a cart at the Eveleigh markets — no sign, no seats, just a queue that kept getting longer. On Saturday 3 April the roaster gets a front door.

Everything is roasted in this room, twelve kilos at a time, and nothing is poured more than three weeks off the roast. Founder Dario Venn still signs every bag by hand.

Good To Know

Before you ask

What if it rains?

The ribbon cutting moves under the awning and the party continues inside. Sydney weather has never cancelled a coffee yet.

How do I get there?

Street parking on Crown Street and the streets behind it; buses run along Crown Street and Central station is a ten-minute walk.

Can I bring kids or a dog?

Both, gladly. Dogs are welcome on the footpath seating, kids are welcome everywhere — the first pour waits for no one, though.

How does the first-hundred gift work?

RSVP as Attending and you take the next number in line. Show your guest-card code at the counter on opening morning and the numbered bag is yours.
