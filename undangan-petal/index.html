Gifts, gently

A book beats a card

The one tradition we keep

Instead of a card, bring a children’s book — any book, new or loved — and write your name inside the cover. It becomes the first shelf of Baby Bennett’s library, and Clara reads the inscriptions on the hard nights.

Wishes for the baby

Good questions

Before you ask June

Can I bring my children?

Babies in arms, always. Older children are loved but the conservatory is small and the guest list is the 40 seats — Clara will celebrate with them at the christening.

What if I can only stay an hour?

Come anyway. Brunch is at eleven and the cake is at half past twelve — even one hour of you is a gift.

Is there a theme for gifts?

The book tradition above, plus the registries. The nursery is sage, butter and clay rose if you are choosing colours.

Who is hosting?

June Bennett, Clara’s sister. Every reply on this page goes to her list — she is the one counting the 40 chairs.
