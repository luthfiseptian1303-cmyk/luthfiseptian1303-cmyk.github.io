The kitchen

“We cook the way a story is told — nothing arrives before its moment, and nothing stays past it.”

EMBER began in 2019 as a two-night-a-week supper club in a Bermondsey railway arch. The room has grown up — green walls, copper light, forty-two covers — but the rule from those nights still runs the kitchen: every dish is a chapter, and the menu is read in order.

Chef Ada Whitmore's cooking is built on open flame and short supply lines. Fish lands from Brixham at dawn, duck comes from a single farm in Suffolk, and the chocolate is one estate in Ecuador, bought whole and ground in-house.

Private dining

The Copper Room

Behind the cellar door: one copper-topped table, ten seats, its own sommelier and a door that closes. The tasting menu is served as one sitting, at your pace, and the kitchen times the duck to your speeches — not the other way round.

For parties of six or more the room carries a £40 room fee, added to the bill on the night. Nothing is taken in advance.

Questions

Before you book

Is there a dress code?

No. Most guests land somewhere around smart-casual, the counter sees the occasional suit, and nobody has ever been turned away for a jumper. Come as the version of yourself that enjoys dinner.

Can you cook for my dietary requirements?

Yes — tell us when you book and the kitchen rebuilds the affected courses properly, including a full vegetarian and vegan tasting menu written by the kitchen, not improvised on the night. Every allergen is handled at the pass, never at the table.

What is the cancellation policy?

Life happens: cancel or move your reservation free of charge up to 24 hours before your sitting, from this page or by phone. Inside 24 hours we only ask that you call — the table is almost always re-seated and no fee applies.

How do I get there, and where do I park?

We are at 14 Bruton Place, off Berkeley Square. Green Park station (Piccadilly, Jubilee, Victoria) is a four-minute walk; the nearest car park is the NCP on Carrington Street, six minutes away. Taxis can set down at the door.
