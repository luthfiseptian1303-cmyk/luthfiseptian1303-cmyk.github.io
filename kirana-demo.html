Good to know

Questions.

Do I need to book, or can I just walk in?

Both work. Walk-ins take a number from the live queue on this page; a booking guarantees your stylist and your exact time.

How does the queue estimate work?

Each room moves at its own pace, so the estimate counts the guests ahead of you in that room and multiplies by its average treatment time. It updates live — and when your number is called, you have 10 minutes to check in at the desk.

What if I run late for a booking?

We hold your slot for 15 minutes. After that it goes to the walk-in queue and we will fit you into the next opening — you never lose the visit, only the exact time.

How do I pay?

At the salon, after your treatment — cards, QRIS and cash are all fine. Nothing is ever charged on this page.
